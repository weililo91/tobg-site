隱私權政策

TOBG — Taiwan Outdoor Bouldering Guide（台灣戶外抱石社群數位指南）

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日　·　版本：1.0
開發者：Wei-Li Lo（羅偉立）　·　App Bundle ID：com.weililo.tobg
聯絡：weililo91@gmail.com

TOBG 是一款戶外抱石的社群共享App。你需要建立帳號才能貢獻內容；你上傳的岩場資料會先經過我們審核，通過後公開給所有使用者瀏覽。本政策說明我們蒐集哪些資料、如何使用，以及你如何刪除它們。

一、帳號與登入

你可以使用下列任一方式註冊與登入：電子郵件＋密碼、Sign in with Apple、Google 登入。

  • 我們蒐集你的電子郵件地址，用於建立帳號、登入驗證與必要的通知。
  • 使用 Sign in with Apple 時，你可選擇以 Apple 的私密轉發信箱隱藏真實信箱；我們僅收到該轉發地址。
  • 使用 Google 登入時，我們僅取得驗證所需的基本識別資訊（如電子郵件與帳號識別碼）。
  • 你的暱稱與頭像由你自行在 App 內設定，屬於你的個人檔案資料。

二、你上傳與建立的內容

當你貢獻內容時，下列資料會上傳至我們的伺服器：岩場與分區名稱、進場點 GPS 座標、石頭照片、路線 Topo 與難度、文字描述與筆記。

請注意：這些內容在我們審核通過後會公開顯示給所有使用者。請勿在其中放入你不希望公開的個人或敏感資訊（例如他人隱私、私人地點）。

在審核通過之前，你的投稿僅你本人與管理者可見。

三、App 權限與用途

📷 相機／照片

用於拍攝或選取石頭與岩場照片。你選擇用於投稿的照片會上傳至我們的雲端儲存，並在審核通過後公開顯示。

📍 位置

用於在地圖上手動釘選進場點座標，以及檢查附近是否已有岩場以避免重複。我們不會在背景持續追蹤你的位置；你釘選並送出的座標會作為岩場內容的一部分上傳與公開。

四、資料儲存與處理者

  • 我們使用 Supabase 作為後端服務，提供帳號驗證、資料庫與檔案儲存（Supabase Storage）。你的帳號資料與上傳內容儲存於 Supabase 代管的雲端基礎設施。
  • 資料透過 HTTPS 加密傳輸。
  • Apple 與 Google 僅在你選擇以其方式登入時，作為登入身分提供者參與驗證。

五、第三方分析與追蹤

TOBG 目前未整合任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK，也不為了廣告目的追蹤你跨 App 的行為（不涉及 Apple ATT 定義的追蹤）。未來預留條款：若日後導入分析或崩潰回報工具（例如用於改善穩定度），我們將更新本政策，並於法律要求時取得你的同意。

六、資料一覽

電子郵件帳號建立與登入驗證；與你的身分連結。
個人檔案（暱稱／頭像）你自行設定，用於在社群中顯示。
使用者內容岩場名稱、GPS 座標、石頭照片、路線等；審核後公開。
識別碼帳號識別碼，用於關聯你的內容與個人檔案。

七、資料保存與刪除（含帳號刪除）

  • 你可隨時在 App 內編輯或刪除自己建立的內容。
  • 你可於 App 內的「設定 → 刪除帳號」永久刪除你的帳號。刪除後，你的帳號、電子郵件與個人檔案資料將自我們的系統移除。
  • 你已審核通過、公開於社群的貢獻內容（岩場、路線等），在你刪除帳號後可能以匿名化形式保留，以維持社群指南的完整性；若你希望一併移除這些內容，可來信請求。
  • 你也可以隨時來信 weililo91@gmail.com 請求存取或刪除你的個人資料。

八、兒童隱私

TOBG 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會蓄意蒐集兒童的個人資料。若我們得知誤蒐集了兒童資料，將儘速刪除。

九、政策變更

我們可能因功能演進而更新本政策。重大變更將更新本頁上方的「生效日期」。建議你不定期回訪查看最新版本。

十、聯絡我們

如對本隱私權政策有任何疑問或資料請求，請聯絡：
weililo91@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

TOBG — Taiwan Outdoor Bouldering Guide

Effective date: October 21, 2026　·　Version: 1.0
Developer: Wei-Li Lo　·　App Bundle ID: com.weililo.tobg
Contact: weililo91@gmail.com

TOBG is a community-sharing app for outdoor bouldering. You create an account to contribute content; crag data you upload is reviewed by us and, once approved, is made publicly visible to all users. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how you can delete it.

1. Accounts & Sign-in

You can register and sign in with any of: email + password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-in.

  • We collect your email address to create your account, verify sign-in, and send necessary notifications.
  • With Sign in with Apple you may use Apple's private relay email to hide your real address; we only receive that relay address.
  • With Google Sign-in we receive only the basic identifiers needed for authentication (such as email and an account ID).
  • Your nickname and avatar are set by you in the app as part of your profile.

2. Content You Upload & Create

When you contribute, the following is uploaded to our servers: crag and area names, approach-point GPS coordinates, boulder photos, route topos and grades, and text notes.

Please note: once approved by us, this content is publicly visible to all users. Do not include personal or sensitive information you don't want made public (e.g. others' privacy, private locations).

Before approval, your submission is visible only to you and the administrator.

3. Permissions & How They Are Used

📷 Camera / Photos

Used to take or pick boulder and crag photos. Photos you choose to submit are uploaded to our cloud storage and shown publicly once approved.

📍 Location

Used to manually drop an approach-point pin on the map and to check for nearby existing crags to avoid duplicates. We do not track your location in the background; coordinates you pin and submit are uploaded and published as part of the crag content.

4. Data Storage & Processors

  • We use Supabase as our backend for authentication, database, and file storage (Supabase Storage). Your account data and uploaded content are stored on Supabase-managed cloud infrastructure.
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Apple and Google act as sign-in identity providers only when you choose to sign in with them.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

TOBG currently integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps for advertising (no "tracking" as defined by Apple's ATT). Reserved clause: if we later add analytics or crash-reporting tools (e.g. to improve stability), we will update this policy and obtain your consent where legally required.

6. Data at a Glance

Email addressAccount creation & sign-in; linked to your identity.
Profile (nickname/avatar)Set by you; shown within the community.
User contentCrag names, GPS, boulder photos, routes; public after approval.
IdentifiersAccount ID used to link your content and profile.

7. Data Retention & Deletion (incl. Account Deletion)

  • You can edit or delete content you created at any time in the app.
  • You can permanently delete your account in-app via Settings → Delete Account. After deletion, your account, email, and profile data are removed from our systems.
  • Content you contributed that was already approved and made public (crags, routes, etc.) may be retained in anonymized form to preserve the integrity of the community guide; email us if you want this content removed as well.
  • You may also email weililo91@gmail.com at any time to request access to or deletion of your personal data.

8. Children's Privacy

TOBG is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn we have collected a child's data, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features evolve. Material changes will be reflected in the "Effective date" at the top. Please check back periodically.

10. Contact Us

For any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact:
weililo91@gmail.com
